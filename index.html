How it works

You make the creative calls. Claude drives the editor and your phone.

1
Put your files in a folder

Your raw takes, the images you want on screen, a song, and a reference reel if you're copying a format.

2
Tell Claude what you want

Structure, what's on screen when, the music, the length. The first video is a conversation; after that it remembers your format.

3
Review the cut

The clean video lands in auto-edit's Review tab. Approve it, or say exactly what to change.

4
Captions, headers, post

It runs the video through Instagram Edits on your phone and shares it from Instagram, as a normal or trial reel, on your schedule.

Why it's different

Built around what actually gets reach.

Native Edits text

Captions and headers are real Instagram Edits text in the app's own fonts, not burned-in overlays.

Posted from Instagram

Every reel is shared from the Instagram app on your phone, never uploaded through an API.

Every pause cut

Dead air and false starts are detected word by word and removed, and every word you meant to say is kept.

Nothing posts unapproved

Finished videos wait in Review until you approve them.

4K60 in, 4K60 out

High-resolution footage stays high-resolution through the edit, the Edits export and the post.

Works with your AI

Claude Desktop (one click), Claude Code, and ChatGPT through a secure tunnel.

Install

About two minutes for the app. The iPhone connection is a one-time setup that Claude walks you through.

Download and drag to Applications

Open auto-edit.dmg and drag the auto-edit icon onto the Applications folder.

Allow it to open (first launch only)

This beta isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before opening it. Open auto-edit, click Done on the warning, then go to

System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → Open Anyway

and confirm. You only do this once.

Connect Claude and follow Get started

The auto-edit window opens on Get started. Click Connect → Add to Claude Desktop, then tell Claude: "Run setup_status and walk me through anything that isn't ready." It finishes the setup with you, including the iPhone.

Prefer the terminal? The server also installs on its own: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LochlanMacQueen/auto-edit/main/install.sh | bash

Before you start

What you'll need.

  • An Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 26 (the editor needs it)
  • An iPhone with Instagram Edits and Instagram installed and logged into the account you post from (Developer Mode on, plugged in by USB)
  • Xcode, once, to connect the phone (free from the App Store; Claude handles the rest)
  • Claude Desktop or Claude Code (or ChatGPT through a tunnel)

Questions

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

This beta is signed on this machine but not yet notarized through Apple's Developer ID program, so Gatekeeper asks you to confirm the first time. Use Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. The source is public if you'd rather build it yourself.

Will it post without me?

Only after you approve. Finished cuts wait in the Review tab, and a reel is shared only after you approve it there, at the time you chose.

Why does it need my iPhone?

Captions and headers are added in the real Instagram Edits app and the reel is shared from Instagram. auto-edit drives those apps on your phone over USB. Keep the phone unlocked and on a Focus mode while it works, so notifications don't interrupt it.

Where do my videos go?

Everything stays on your Mac and your phone. Projects and outputs live in your own folders, and settings live in ~/AutoEdit. The only network access is downloading the speech model once and posting to your Instagram.

Is it free? Is it open source?

Yes to both. auto-edit is GPL-3.0. The editor is a fork of Palmier Pro's open-source release.
