how it works

you make the creative calls. your ai drives the editor and your phone.

1
drop your takes in a folder

raw clips, the pictures you want on screen, a song. that's the whole setup.

2
tell claude what you want

hook → sections → call to action, what's on screen when, how long. it remembers your format.

3
review the cut

every pause gone, every word kept. approve it or say exactly what to change.

4
it posts from your phone

native instagram edits captions + headers, shared from instagram. normal or trial reel, on your schedule.

install in two minutes

free, no account, no subscription. your videos never leave your mac and phone.

Install auto-edit
1
download & drag to Applications

open auto-edit.dmg and drag the icon onto your Applications folder.

2
allow it once

this beta isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks the first time. open auto-edit, click Done, then:

System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway
3
connect claude & follow "get started"

click Connect → Add to Claude Desktop, then say "run setup_status and walk me through anything that isn't ready." it sets up your iPhone with you.
