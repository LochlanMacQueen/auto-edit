Step 1 of 8

1 · Let Claude set up your Mac and phone

You need a Mac with the latest Xcode, an iPhone in Developer Mode, Palmier Pro, and Claude Code. Don’t do it by hand — copy this prompt, open Claude Code with this repo’s folder attached, paste it, and Claude walks you through every step and checks it.

Read it

Come back here when Claude says setup is done (≈30 minutes, once).

2 · Put your files in a folder

One folder per format, e.g. ~/Reels/my-format. Drop in:

  • your raw takes (one take per sentence or section works best)
  • the images or screenshots you want on screen
  • the song — it gets baked into the video
  • a reference reel (URL or file) if you’re copying a format

Claude names and organises everything; you don’t need to sort it.

3 · Give it to Claude and tell it exactly what you want

Copy this prompt into Claude Code (repo folder attached), then say where the folder is and describe the edit in as much depth as you can: the structure, what’s on screen when, the music, the length, what must never happen.

Read it

The more specific you are here, the fewer questions later.

4 · Review what Claude made

Claude cuts every pause, places your images, builds the video in Palmier Pro with the sound baked in, and hands it back to you. Watch it. Say what’s wrong, as precisely as you can — Claude fixes it in place and returns it again.

Repeat until it’s right. That version becomes the template for every video after it.

5 · Send it to Edits and tell it the text

Once you approve the cut, Claude runs it through Instagram Edits on your phone: auto-captions plus your headers. Tell it what text goes on screen, when, and how it should look (font, size, colour, position).

Claude will pause and ask. Answer specifically. Sound is already in the video from Palmier — nothing is added in Edits.

After this first video, every one after it is a single command.

6 · Have it post

Tell Claude to post — normal reel or trial reel, now or spaced out. It shares from the Instagram app on your phone and logs the time.

That’s it. Reopen this guide any time from Setup guide in the top bar.
